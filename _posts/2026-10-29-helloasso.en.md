Since its launch in April 2026, _Sharly-Chess.com_ has been adopted by numerous clubs, departmental committees, and leagues — even being used at the French Championship in Vichy this past August.

Thank you for your trust and your feedback, specifically:
- the simple registration process for players,
- the ability to view registrations in real time,
- online check-in,
- and, most importantly, the time saved by organizers and arbiters, thanks to the seamless integration between the _Sharly-Chess.com_ registration platform and the _Sharly Chess_ arbitration software.

You also shared that the commission charged on each registration placed a significant burden on club budgets. Starting today, we are introducing two updates to address this:

- **Fixed registration credits are replacing the previous variable commission model**;
- **We are pleased to announce a partnership with HelloAsso**: French associations can now collect registration fees directly into their own _HelloAsso_ accounts, with no commissions or card processing fees.

## 1. Registration Credits

- Each paid registration consumes one fixed **credit**, regardless of the registration fee amount or the payment method used.
- **Free events remain free**.
- Sales of products (meals, refreshments, etc.) do not consume any credits.
- If an registration is cancelled and a refund is issued, the credit used are returned to you;
- You can purchase credits via your organizer dashboard (on the **Credits** page) using a credit card or bank transfer. An invoice is provided for every purchase.
- The cost of registration credits is fixed, starting at €0.25 ([price details](https://sharly-chess.com/en/pricing)).

**Existing Events**

New events will use the registration credit system. Events created prior to today will continue to operate under the commission-based model until you switch them to the credit-based system by clicking the **Switch to credits** button on the event page. Registrations processed before this switch remain unchanged (the commission still applies, and no credits are consumed); however, every subsequent paid registration will consume one credit. This change is permanent and is required to enable features such as *HelloAsso*, on-site payments, or bank transfers.

## 2. *HelloAsso* – A Partner of *Sharly-Chess.com*

We are delighted to partner with *HelloAsso*, a payment solution designed for French non-profit organizations and already used by many French chess clubs. Thanks to this partnership, *HelloAsso* is now fully integrated into *Sharly-Chess.com*:

- **Funds from players are deposited directly into your organization’s *HelloAsso* account.** - **No commissions or card processing fees**: *HelloAsso* is free for French associations; it is funded solely through voluntary contributions that players may choose to leave at the time of payment.
- **Automatic registration confirmation**, with payments and refunds tracked directly on *Sharly-Chess.com*.
- **Connect in just a few clicks**: Go to the **Payments** page in your organizer dashboard, click **Connect *HelloAsso***, and then authorize access via your *HelloAsso* account.

*HelloAsso* is available exclusively to French associations. Does your association not have an account yet? You can create one for free here: https://auth.helloasso.com/inscription/association/1?from=SharlyChess

## 3. Other payment methods remain available

For each event, you can choose how players make their payments:

- **_HelloAsso_**, for French non-profit organizations;
- **_Stripe_ (credit/debit card)**, as is currently the case: only _Stripe_ fees apply (€0.25 + 1.5% per transaction), and registrations are confirmed automatically;
- **On-site, or via another method (bank transfer, etc.)**: you confirm the registration once the payment has been received.

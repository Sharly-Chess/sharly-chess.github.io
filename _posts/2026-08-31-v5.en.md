Version 5.0 of _Sharly Chess_ is a major evolution of the application, introducing:
- support for team competitions;
- better compliance with _FIDE_ rules regarding player titles and the calculation of the norms required to get them;
- a partially renewed web interface that is more intuitive and, above all, much faster;
- new features for you to explore;
- simpler and standardized application updates on Windows and MacOS.

## Team competitions

You can now run full **team events**, where players compete in teams, teams are paired against each other, and standings are kept in team scores. The release adds:

- six team **pairing systems** — Swiss, Round-Robin, Two-game Round-Robin, Scheveningen, Two-game Scheveningen and Molter;
- **team scoring** with match points and game points, and a set of team tie-breaks;
- team **documents and screens** — match sheets, the Berger grid, team rankings, and team check-in / match / ranking displays.

👉 See the new [Team events]({% link docs/team-tournaments/index.en.md %}) section to get started.

### Molter, for everyone

The **Molter system** is a fixed-table method for small team events over a few rounds — everyone plays every round, nobody sits out. It is named after Charles Molter, whose 1978 documents were turned into ready-to-use tables in 1991 by international arbiter Jean-Claude Templeur and distributed by the _Fédération Française des Échecs_; it has been little known outside France. _Sharly Chess_ now **generates Molter tables automatically** for any supported size, bringing it to organisers everywhere.

👉 Read [Molter tables]({% link docs/team-tournaments/molter-tables.en.md %}) to learn when and why to use it.

### French team competitions

For French events, the [_FFE_ plugin]({% link docs/plugins/france/ffe.en.md %}) adds rule sets for the official team competitions:
- Jean-Claude Loubatière Cup,
- Women's championship (N1F / N2F),
- the Mixed Cup,
along with the _Loubatière_ pairing sheet.

## User Interface

The user interface features numerous improvements:
- The console has been revamped and now includes new tabs.
- The web interface has been partially redesigned to be even **more intuitive**. Notably, you can now use a "list" view instead of the standard "card" view on administration pages (events, tournaments, prizes, screens, menus, timers, etc.).
- You will see for yourself that this new interface is much faster than the previous version.

## _FIDE_ titles

Players _FIDE_ titles are now correctly handled:
- Players now have separate _FIDE_ titles for the open and women titles, so a player can hold both at once (for example _IM_ and _WIM_).
- Titles are displayed intelligently: both are shown when they matter (e.g. _FM_ and _WIM_), but the women title is hidden when the open title supersedes it (e.g. _GM_ instead of _GM/WGM_).
- When the _FIDE_ database is installed locally, players looked up from it (including via an _FFE_ search) have their women title filled in automatically.

## Pairing systems and accelerations

- New **Custom accelerated system** (acceleration defined round by round)
- New **Initial score accelerated system** (virtual points individually granted to players)
- Pairing settings can be prepared prior to pairing and viewed at any time
- Arbiters are warned when pairing settings leads to top acceleration groups with an odd number of players at round #1

## Rankings

The points are now a ranking criterion in their own right, listed alongside the tie-breaks.
 
Bonus / penalty points can be given to players (TRF26 299 record).

## Fixed tables

The handling of fixed tables has been improved in several ways:
- The tables that the players would have been assigned to are no longer left empty.
- You can now assign any table number as a fixed table without having a duplicate table appearing.
- Changing fixed table assignment mid tournament is now correctly handled (previous round table assignments are kept).
- Pairings can be sorted by pairing order or board number on the Pairings tab, the Pairings document and the Pairings screens.

### Fixed tables in French competitions

For French tournaments, the [_FFE_ plugin]({% link docs/plugins/france/ffe.en.md %}) maintains compatibility with the display of table numbers on the _FFE_ website:
- By default, we still leave the orignal table empty when the players are moved to a fixed table number
- In this compatibility mode, duplicate tables still occur when fixed table numbers are inside the normal table range
This behaviour can be changed in the _FFE_ plugin section of event's configuration.

## Norm reports

_FIDE_ compliance of norm reports has been improved:
- Norm reports generated before the end of the tournament now assume that players will play the remaining rounds for the purposes of displaying 1.5.6a and 1.4.3d.
- Title norm calculations now take women titles into account: opponents holding a WGM or WIM title are correctly counted towards the 1.4.5 and 1.4.3d requirements, even when they also hold an open title.

## Prizes

The management of prizes has been improved:
- A quick way to generate prize categories for age categories and rating ranges has been added.
- Duplication prize categories are now added immediately after the original.
- Prizes are duplicated when duplicating a tournament.

## Screens

Screen families have been renamed **Multi-Screens**.

Menu support has been completely redesigned for a better experience and existing screens have been improved:
- Check-in screens now correctly use the configure column count.
- Vertical lines are now displayed between columns.

A new **Chess 960** plugin allows you to display start positions, selected by the arbiter or determined by a draw.

## Misc

- The pairings document now has an option to include a federation column.
- HTML documents can be uploaded to a custom location.
- Staff members can now be assigned as arbiters, separately from chief and deputy arbiter roles, and are included in tournament documents and _Chess-Results_ exports.

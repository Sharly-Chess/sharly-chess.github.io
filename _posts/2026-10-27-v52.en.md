Version 5.2 of _Sharly Chess_ is now available. With this version, _Sharly Chess_ is ready to be submitted to the _FIDE_ for compliance testing as soon as the _FIDE_ opens to new submissions.

The main additions are:

- a **_FIDE_ mode** that keeps Swiss tournaments within the _FIDE_ regulations
- the **rating lists of fifteen national federations**, and a new way of **choosing the ratings** a tournament uses
- **round publication**: pairings stay private until you publish them
- **custom schedules** for round robins
- documents that can be saved as **PDF** and **Word** files
- **automatic backups** of your events
- numerous other improvements throughout the application

## _FIDE_ mode

Swiss tournaments now run in **_FIDE_ mode** by default. _Sharly Chess_ refuses the actions the _FIDE_ regulations prohibit, warns you before the ones they do not describe, and keeps a log of every change that falls outside the _FIDE_ rules or recommendations.

- **Correcting results**: each correction to the previous round is confirmed and logged, and older rounds cannot be changed. A wrong result found in an older round can still be corrected in the rating report, without changing the pairings or the standings.
- **Pairing by hand**: when you change the pairings of a round by hand, _Sharly Chess_ warns about repeated games, prohibited pairings and colour rules, and checks your pairings against the pairing engine before the next round can be paired.
- **Settings**: once the tournament has started, the points and the pairing-allocated bye are fixed, and changing the number of rounds or the tie-breaks needs a double confirmation.
- **The log**: every logged event is shown on the pairings page, with its round and date, and written as comments in the TRF file sent to the _FIDE_.

A tournament can leave _FIDE_ mode at any time, but once its first round is paired it can never return to it, and the TRF records the round at which it left.

The TRF export is now named after its use: _TRF26 (ITDX)_ while the tournament is in progress, and _TRF26 (final)_ for the rating report.

👉 Read the new [_FIDE_ mode]({% link docs/running-an-event/fide-mode.en.md %}) page.

## National rating lists and ratings

Besides the _FIDE_ list, _Sharly Chess_ can now download the rating lists of **fifteen national federations** and convert them on your machine, so that players can be found without an internet connection during the event: Canada, the Czech Republic, Denmark, England, Finland, Germany, Indonesia, Italy, Japan, Malaysia, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Russia, South Africa and Ukraine.

Only the data sources you use are shown: those of your federation are activated with your first event, and the others can be added from the **Data sources** window. A player found in a national list keeps their federation's identifier next to their _FIDE_ ID, and gets their _FIDE_ ratings and titles from the _FIDE_ list.

The way a tournament chooses its players' ratings has been rebuilt:

- each tournament ranks its players on the **first rating they hold** in a sequence of ratings tried — for example the _FIDE_ rating, then the national rating — chosen among ready-made options, the _FIDE_'s defaults first;
- each rating remembers **where it came from** — the list and its date — and the tournament shows whether it is a _FIDE_, national or estimated rating;
- **Update › Player ratings** checks the players' ratings against the latest lists and shows what would change before anything is applied;
- players with no rating in any list are given a rating set by the tournament or the event, 1400 by default.

Tournaments that last **more than 30 days**, such as a club championship over a season, can also be split into periods of at most 30 days, each rated on the ratings in force when it is played, as the _FIDE_ requires.

👉 See [Data sources]({% link docs/player-databases/index.en.md %}) and [Tournaments over 30 days]({% link docs/running-an-event/rating-periods.en.md %}).

## Publishing the rounds

New pairings are now **private until you publish them**. Once a round is paired, you can check its pairings, correct them and print them, while the screens and the online services (_Sharly-Chess.com_, _Chess-Results_, the _FFE_ website, custom uploads) still show the previous round. When everything is ready, click **Publish**.

No more pairings going out with a mistake in them: a round can be unpublished as long as it has no results, to correct it out of sight.

👉 See [Publishing the Pairings]({% link docs/running-an-event/managing-pairings.en.md %}#publishing-the-pairings).

## Custom round-robin schedules

Round robins, individual and team, can now be played on a **schedule you build yourself**, round by round and board by board, or start from the Berger tables and be edited after pairing.

_Sharly Chess_ checks the schedule as you build it — each pair meeting once (or twice), no three games in a row with the same colour — and lists the rules it breaks. If players sat at the wrong boards, the games already played can be corrected to match what was really played.

Double round robins can also reverse the last two rounds of the first cycle, as the _FIDE_ recommends, to avoid three games in a row with the same colour.

👉 See [The schedule]({% link docs/running-an-event/pairing-systems.en.md %}#the-schedule).

## PDF and Word documents

Every document now has buttons at the top of the page to **print** it and to download it as a **PDF** file, without going through the print dialog. Documents with fields to fill in, such as the norm report and the _FFE_ forms, can also be downloaded as **Word** files, with the values you entered, ready to be completed and sent.

## Automatic backups

_Sharly Chess_ now **backs up your events at key moments** — before a round is paired or unpaired, before a result is changed, before players are distributed, before an update — so that the arbiter can quickly return an event to an earlier state if needed. A backup can be restored over the event, or beside it as a new event. The backups are kept beside the data folder rather than inside it, and you can choose your own backup folder.

On Windows, the data now lives in the local application data rather than in the Documents folder, where cloud services such as OneDrive could lock the events while _Sharly Chess_ was writing to them. The data is moved on the first start.

## Miscellaneous

Among the other improvements:

- several computers can synchronise the same event with _Sharly-Chess.com_ more reliably
- games can be recorded as **adjourned**, counting as draws until their result is entered
- a **name format** setting chooses how players' names are displayed (Last First, First Last…)
- players can be marked as not eligible for byes
- a new **PGN (all rounds)** export, and PGN files now carry the results, round dates, time control and _FIDE_ IDs
- the rules of the accelerated pairing systems can be printed
- _FFE_ arbiter titles follow the 2026 reform

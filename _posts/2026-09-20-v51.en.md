Version 5.1 of _Sharly Chess_ is now available, with the following additions:

- a **knock-out** pairing system, for individual and team events
- a **visual editor** for place cards
- application settings and plugins gathered in the **main application window**
- an administration interface that works on a **phone**
- **filters** for the player search
- **club separation** when distributing players
- better _FIDE_ compliance for **round robins**
- numerous other improvements throughout the application

## Knock-out tournaments

The biggest addition in version 5.1 is the **knock-out** (cup) pairing system, for both individual and team events.

Players — or teams — are seeded into a bracket: the loser of each match is out, the winner moves on, and the last one standing wins. The number of rounds follows from the size of the field, the top seeds skip round one when the field is not a power of two, and the ranking is by the round reached: winner, runner-up, third place, out in round _N_…

Four variations are available:

- **Single elimination** — one game per match, one loss and you are out.
- **Single elimination with two-game matches** — each match over two games with colours reversed, decided on aggregate.
- **Double elimination** — a first loss drops the participant into a lower bracket; a second loss eliminates. The winners of the two brackets meet in a Grand Final, optionally with a **reset** game if the lower-bracket champion wins it.
- **Double elimination with two-game matches**.

Because chess games can be drawn, a knock-out needs a way to decide level matches. _Sharly Chess_ 5.1 introduces **advancement tie-breaks**: the tie-breaks configured on a knock-out tournament do not rank the standings, they decide who goes through — rating, pairing number, games won, performance… — and a **Manual** tie-break lets the arbiter designate the winner of a play-off (Armageddon, blitz games…) played over the board.

The settings cover what a cup organiser expects: a **third-place playoff**, the **colour assignment** rule (alternate colours, higher seed with White, or drawing of lots), and **grouped seeding** by club, federation or affiliation, so that club-mates play each other first and the two strongest groups can only meet in the final — with a live preview of the resulting bracket.

A new **Knock-out Bracket** document prints the bracket diagram, or the schedule of matches round by round.

👉 Read the new [Knock-out]({% link docs/running-an-event/knockout.en.md %}) page to get started.

## Place card editor

Place cards no longer require editing template files by hand: version 5.1 includes a **visual editor**.

From the **Templates** button of the Documents dialog, you can create a new template or duplicate a built-in one, then design it on screen: add text and images, drag them into place, choose fonts, sizes, colours and alignments, and preview the card with sample data. Templates can be **imported and exported** to share them with other organisers.

The editor writes the same TOML templates as before, so existing custom templates keep working and can still be fine-tuned by hand.

👉 See the [Place Cards Customization Guide]({% link docs/documents/place-cards.en.md %}).

## The main application window

The **language**, **federation** and **date format** settings have moved from the browser to the **main application window** — the window that opens when you launch _Sharly Chess_ — alongside the data folder and update settings.

**Plugins** are now installed and uninstalled from the same window, from a new Plugins page listing the available plugins with their type, federation, dependencies and documentation.

The **event export** (SCE) has moved too, from the tournament tab to the event's configuration window.

👉 See [Accessing the User Interface]({% link docs/getting-started/accessing-ui.en.md %}) and [Plugins]({% link docs/plugins/index.en.md %}).

## Administration from a phone

The administration interface now adapts to **phone screens**: pairings can be checked and results entered from a smartphone in the playing hall.

## Player search filters

The player search can now be **narrowed** by federation, gender, age category and club — plus licence and league for _FFE_ searches. The filters can be filled in one go from the **criteria** of any tournament of the event, which makes registering players section by section much quicker.

## Player distribution

When distributing players across balanced tournaments, players of the **same club** can now be **separated** as much as possible. A reminder to update the players' data from the latest rating lists is shown before distributing them — and before generating place cards.

## Round robins

Two improvements for round-robin tournaments:

- the **_FIDE_ 6.6 participation rule** can be enabled on the tournament: a player (or team) who withdrew or was expelled having completed less than half of their games is dropped from the final standings and their games are annulled;
- in individual round robins, when moving on to the next round, _Sharly Chess_ now asks whether the current round should be **marked as completed**, and warns you if boards are still without a result.

## Miscellaneous

Among the other improvements:

- results entered on an administration pairings screen are now notified immediately to the other administration screens, as results entered on an input screen already were
- the rating difference is now sent to _Chess-Results_
- team tournaments paired on fixed tables (Molter) print the pairings document in place of the match sheets, and their team ranking now shows the game points and tie-breaks
- the team size limit is enforced when importing players from a CSV file

Version 5.0 of _Sharly Chess_ is a major evolution of the application, with the following additions:

- full support for team competitions
- championships combining tournaments across multiple events
- better compliance with _FIDE_ rules regarding player titles and title norms
- a partially redesigned web interface that is more intuitive and, above all, much faster
- new pairing and acceleration features
- numerous other improvements throughout the application
- simpler and standardised application updates on Windows and macOS

## Team competitions

The biggest addition in version 5 is full support for **team events**.

Players can now compete as members of teams, teams can be paired against each other, and rankings can be calculated using team scores and dedicated tie-breaks.

_Sharly Chess_ 5.0 introduces:

- six team **pairing systems** — Swiss, Round-Robin, Two-game Round-Robin, Scheveningen, Two-game Scheveningen and Molter
- **team scoring** using match points and game points, together with a range of team tie-breaks
- team **documents and screens** — including match sheets, Berger grids, team rankings, and team check-in, match and ranking displays

👉 See the new [Team events]({% link docs/team-tournaments/index.en.md %}) section to get started.

### Molter, for everyone

The **Molter system** is a fixed-table pairing method designed for small team events played over a limited number of rounds: everyone plays in every round and nobody sits out.

It is named after Charles Molter, whose 1978 documents were turned into ready-to-use tables in 1991 by international arbiter Jean-Claude Templeur and distributed by the _Fédération Française des Échecs_. Despite its usefulness, the system has remained little known outside France.

_Sharly Chess_ now **generates Molter tables automatically** for any supported number of teams, making the system easily available to organisers everywhere.

👉 Read [Molter tables]({% link docs/team-tournaments/molter-tables.en.md %}) to learn when and why to use it.

### French team competitions

For French events, the [_FFE_ plugin]({% link docs/plugins/france/ffe.en.md %}) adds dedicated rule sets for several official team competitions:

- Jean-Claude Loubatière Cup
- Women's Championship (N1F / N2F)
- Mixed Cup

It also provides the official _Loubatière_ pairing sheet.

## Championships

Version 5 also introduces **championships**, allowing results from several tournaments to be combined into an overall competition.

A championship can span **multiple tournaments and multiple events**, whether individual or team-based. This makes it possible, for example, to organise a season-long series of tournaments and automatically determine an overall winner.

_Sharly Chess_:

- automatically aggregates competitors across the different tournaments
- calculates the overall championship ranking
- supports a wide range of configurable ranking criteria and tie-break systems
- generates partial rankings based on categories such as age, gender, federation, etc.

Individual tournaments remain independent while contributing towards the overall championship.

## User interface

The user interface has received numerous improvements:

- The console has been revamped and now includes new tabs.
- Administration pages can now use a **list view** instead of the traditional card view for events, tournaments, prizes, screens, menus, timers and more.
- Numerous interface elements have been redesigned to make the application more intuitive.
- The web interface is now significantly faster than in previous versions.

## _FIDE_ titles

_FIDE_ player titles are now handled accurately.

- Players now have separate _FIDE_ titles for the open and women's categories, allowing a player to hold both simultaneously (for example _IM_ and _WIM_).
- Titles are displayed intelligently: both are shown when they are relevant (for example _FM_ and _WIM_), while the women's title is hidden when the open title supersedes it (for example _GM_ rather than _GM/WGM_).
- When the _FIDE_ database is installed locally, players retrieved from it — including through an _FFE_ search — automatically have their women's title filled in.

## Pairing systems and accelerations

Version 5 adds new possibilities for accelerated tournaments:

- a new **Custom accelerated system**, where acceleration can be defined round by round
- a new **Initial score accelerated system**, allowing virtual pairing points to be granted individually to players
- pairing settings can now be prepared before generating the pairings and consulted at any time
- arbiters are warned when the selected pairing settings result in top acceleration groups containing an odd number of players in round 1

## Rankings

Points are now treated as a **ranking criterion in their own right**, alongside the available tie-break systems.

This provides greater flexibility over how tournament rankings are calculated.

Bonus and penalty points can also be awarded to players, with support for the TRF26 `299` record.

## Fixed tables

The handling of fixed tables has been substantially improved:

- tables that players would normally have occupied are no longer unnecessarily left empty
- any table number can now be assigned as a fixed table without causing an additional duplicate table to appear
- fixed-table assignments can be changed during a tournament while preserving the table assignments from previous rounds
- pairings can be sorted either by pairing order or by board number on the Pairings tab, Pairings document and Pairings screens

### Fixed tables in French competitions

For French tournaments, the [_FFE_ plugin]({% link docs/plugins/france/ffe.en.md %}) includes a compatibility mode designed to keep table numbering consistent with the _FFE_ website.

By default, the original table is therefore still left empty when players are moved to a fixed table. In this compatibility mode, duplicate table numbers may also occur when fixed table numbers fall within the normal table range.

This behaviour can be changed in the _FFE_ plugin section of the event configuration.

## Norm reports

_FIDE_ compliance of title norm reports has been improved:

- norm reports generated before the end of a tournament now assume that players will play the remaining rounds for the purposes of displaying requirements 1.5.6a and 1.4.3d
- title norm calculations now take women's titles into account: opponents holding a _WGM_ or _WIM_ title are correctly counted towards the 1.4.5 and 1.4.3d requirements, even when they also hold an open title.

## Prizes

Prize management has also been improved:

- prize categories for age groups and rating ranges can now be generated quickly
- duplicated prize categories are inserted immediately after the original
- prizes are automatically duplicated when duplicating a tournament

## Screens

Screen families have been renamed **Multi-Screens**.

Menu support has been completely redesigned for a better experience, and existing screens have also been improved:

- check-in screens now correctly use the configured column count
- vertical separators are now displayed between columns

A new **Chess960** plugin allows starting positions to be displayed, either selected by the arbiter or determined by a draw.

## Miscellaneous

Among many other improvements:

- the Pairings document now has an option to include a federation column
- HTML documents can be uploaded to a custom location
- staff members can now be assigned as arbiters independently of the chief and deputy arbiter roles, and are included in tournament documents and _Chess-Results_ exports

## Update to Version 5

New _Sharly Chess_ users can directly download and install the latest version of the software from [this page]({% link docs/getting-started/index.en.md %}).

For existing _Sharly Chess_ users, the recommended procedure to preserve your events is as follows:

1. launch the currently installed version and accept the update to the latest stable version 4 (4.2.8)
2. launch version 4.2.8 and accept the update to version 5

Since the release of version 4.2 in early June, the _Sharly Chess_ developers have been working hard to bring you version 5 soon, featuring a host of new capabilities:
- **Team competition management** (a highly anticipated feature), allowing you to manage both individual and team events using a single tool. All team competition formats are supported: Swiss system, single and double round-robin, and the Molter system (supporting up to 25 teams, 12 players per team, and 24 rounds!), with rule sets tailored to specific _FFE_ competitions (Jean-Claude Loubatière Cup, Mixed Cup, Women's Interclub);
- **Fully automated application updates**, ensuring seamless data migration from previous versions;
- An improved web interface that is **faster and more intuitive**;
- **Open and women _FIDE_ titles can now be assigned to women**, with both titles taken into account into norm reports;
- **Enhanced management of fixed tables and prizes**;
- **Simplified screen creation**, plus a **new Chess 960 screen type** that displays starting positions to the players;
- Additional features, such as the ability to upload all documents (player lists, pairings, ranking, etc.) to a custom server.

A beta version is already available online, and we are looking for testers to try out these new features. 
Feel free to contact us and join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/WGG87eJzQZ) to get an early look at this new version, we look forward to assisting you! 

In the meantime, you can go ahead and update _Sharly Chess_ to version 4.2.7; this is an essential step for the upcoming transition to version 5.

We wish you a wonderful August and a great French Championship in Vichy if you are lucky enough to be participating, and we look forward to seeing you soon for the official release of _Sharly Chess_ 5.0!

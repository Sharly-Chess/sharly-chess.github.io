# Feeds

A device can show a screen by simply opening its address. But to change what it shows, you then have to go to the device — which is not convenient for a projector on the ceiling or a television at the other end of the hall.

A **feed** solves this. You open the feed once on the device, and from then on you choose what it shows from the arbiter's computer. Any number of devices can follow the same feed: all the televisions of the playing hall, for example, or all the tablets used for result entry.

Feeds are optional, and many events don't need one. But it is often a good idea to start with them: create one feed per kind of device — _Projector_, _Tablets_ — and then create the screens they need.

---

## Creating a feed

On the **Display → Feeds** tab, click **Create a feed** and give it a name that will help you recognise the device, such as _Projector_.

A feed can be **public** or private. A private feed can only be followed by a device signed in with an account that is allowed to see the private screens.

The **Screens designed for this feed** field lists the screens, paged screens, rotators and menus meant for this device. They are offered first when choosing what the feed shows, which keeps the list short; anything else can still be sent to it. The same choice is available from each screen, in its **Designed for** field.

---

## Connecting a device

Click the feed's **Connect a device** button. It shows the feed's address on each of your computer's networks, with a QR code: open the address on the device, or scan the code. The device then shows _Connected. Waiting for a screen…_ until something is sent to the feed.

Players and spectators can also find the public feeds on the event's [Display page]({% link docs/screens/index.en.md %}#visitors), where choosing a feed opens it straight away.

---

## Choosing what a feed shows

A feed shows one thing at a time, and what you send decides how it behaves:

- a **screen** stays on the device;
- a **paged screen** goes through its pages in turn, at its seconds per page or every 15 seconds;
- a **single page** of a paged screen stays on the device;
- a **rotator** goes through its screens in turn;
- a **menu** lets people tap between its screens, which suits the tablets.

There are two ways to send something to a feed:

- from the **Feeds** tab, with the feed's <i class="bi-box-arrow-in-right"></i> **Send a screen to this feed** button. The menu lists what is designed for the feed first, grouped by kind, and everything else under **Other screens**. A paged screen opens a submenu: **All pages in turn**, or one of its pages;
- from the **Screens**, **Rotators** or **Menus** tab, with the <i class="bi-box-arrow-in-right"></i> button of what you want to show, which lists the feeds. A single page of a paged screen is sent from the paged screen's details.

Every device following the feed changes within a few seconds.

---

## Watching the feeds

The **Feeds** tab can show the feeds as a **list**, as **cards**, or as **previews**: a live miniature of what each feed is currently showing, which lets you check all the devices of the hall from the arbiter's table.
